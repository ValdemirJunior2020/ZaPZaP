## How NexusSales AI works
NexusSales AI lets business owners publish product/service catalogs and personal/business chat assistants. Visitors may send text to an AI assistant and optionally submit contact details and appointment requests. Business owners may view transcripts through their authenticated dashboards.

## Categories of information handled (verify against live configuration)
- Account: business name, owner email, business phone, authentication identifiers, profile and subscription status.
- Business data: services, pricing, hours, photos (which may be hosted externally), assistant name/settings and QR attribution.
- Visitor data: chat messages, session timestamps, lead contact details when submitted, appointment requests and selected business/channel references.
- Operational data: request abuse-budget pseudonymous fingerprints, AI diagnostic codes, errors and payment/subscription identifiers where applicable.
- Payments: recurring subscriptions are processed through PayPal; NexusSales does not need to collect full payment card details in its application forms.

## Purposes
To provide AI chat and storefront features, maintain conversations and customer requests for the relevant owner, route and confirm appointment requests, detect abuse, maintain account and subscription status, and respond to security/support requests. ## AI and third-party processing
Messages are transmitted through an authenticated AI gateway and may be processed by a hosted or locally-operated Ollama service; optional third-party failover may be enabled by the operator. Do not promise zero third-party processing without verifying all enabled routes. The service also uses Supabase for data and authentication, PayPal for subscription billing, a hosting provider (currently Vercel), and optional Meta/WhatsApp for connected WhatsApp Business channels. Record the verified processors and data regions before publication.

## Retention and deletion
The current design retains chat transcripts in the owner's account without a fixed automatic expiration period, unless the owner deletes the conversation, deletes their account, or an applicable deletion/legal process requires removal. A chat's 5–30 minute session window limits active interaction; it is not the record retention period. A deleted chat session removes its linked chat messages; a linked customer lead may remain without the transcript. Owners must honor applicable deletion and retention requests from visitors, including cases where a visitor has no registered account. Backups, provider retention and legal hold periods require separate verification.

## Who can access
The respective authenticated business owner may access their business records and conversations. Administrators/service providers may have access to operate and support the platform subject to appropriate controls. Verify any employee access boundaries before publication. Public visitors should not have direct access to private lead/contact records.

## Security
The backend uses authenticated database access, scoped owner operations, HTTPS and usage limits. No online system is risk-free. Do not promise absolute security, guaranteed uptime, or perfect AI accuracy.
